# Portfolio alignment to technology scouting work

This project represents the working outcomes of an early-career technology
scouting role without copying an employer's internal process or claiming access
to proprietary methods. It demonstrates how the work can be structured, reviewed
and reused. It is not a claim that every organization uses this exact workflow.

| Role outcome | Evidence in this project | Deliberate boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Monitor technology domains and market developments | Configurable domains, OpenAlex/GDELT adapters, approved web sources, schedules and source health | Public and synthetic inputs only; provider coverage is not described as exhaustive |
| Capture early signals across H1–H4 | Versioned evidence, transparent score inputs, signal rules and configurable horizons | H1–H4 is an illustrative portfolio method, not Siemens Energy's internal methodology |
| Maintain Technology Radar entries | Searchable profiles, assessment state, review queue, placement history and archived records | AI suggests; only an analyst can approve or move a technology |
| Prepare startup and research interactions | Startup/institution profiles, linked papers, background summaries and questions to ask | Partnerships, funding and patents remain “not verified” until sourced |
| Maintain scouting records and briefings | Central normalized repository, audit log, weekly/monthly reports, PDF/Markdown export | Generated briefings retain provenance and require analyst review |
| Use AI for searchability and synthesis | Bounded retrieval, pgvector/FAISS, structured Gemini output, citation and numeric-claim validation | AI text is interpretation, never promoted to factual evidence |

The project also shows the supporting engineering skills behind those outcomes:
data modeling, API integration, resilient ingestion, deduplication, data quality,
access control, testing, deployment preparation and clear written communication.
These capabilities support the role; they do not imply knowledge of confidential
Siemens Energy systems, assessments or portfolio decisions.

## Suggested portfolio explanation

> I built an evidence-first technology scouting workspace for grid technologies.
> It demonstrates how I would monitor public research and market signals, maintain
> traceable radar entries, prepare organization briefings and use grounded AI to
> make evidence easier to search and reuse. The horizon model and sample data are
> explicitly illustrative, while analyst approval remains the decision point.

That framing lets a recruiter see direct relevance without suggesting that the
application was reverse-engineered from a job description or internal process.
